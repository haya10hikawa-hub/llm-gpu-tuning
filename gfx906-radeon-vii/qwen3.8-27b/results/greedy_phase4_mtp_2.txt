To understand how HBM2 (High Bandwidth Memory 2) limits autoregressive token generation on a GPU, we must first look at the fundamental architecture of modern GPUs and the specific mathematical requirements of Large Language Models (LLMs).

The core of the limitation is the **Memory Wall**. In autoregressive generation, the GPU's arithmetic units (ALUs) are starved for data. This phenomenon is often described as the system being "Memory Bandwidth Bound" rather than "Compute Bound."
