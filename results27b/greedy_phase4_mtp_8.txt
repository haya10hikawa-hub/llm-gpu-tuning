Modern high-end GPUs that use HBM2/HBM2e/HBM3 are all equipped with dedicated tensor/matrix cores (e.g., NVIDIA's Tensor Cores, AMD's Matrix Cores). If you remove the matrix cores from such a GPU, what remains is essentially a general-purpose parallel processor
