

Explain in detail how HBM2 memory bandwidth limits autoregressive token generation on a GPU without matrix cores.

To understand how High Bandwidth Memory (HBM2) limits autoregressive token generation, we must first look at the fundamental shift in the bottleneck during the generation phase compared to the prefill phase.

In the **Prefill phase**, the model processes the entire prompt in parallel. This is a compute-bound operation where the GPU's massive parallelism (via matrix cores) is fully utilized. However, in the **Autoregressive generation phase**, the model generates tokens one by one. For each token, the entire sequence of previous tokens (the context) is processed to predict the next one.
